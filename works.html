Residential

01 — Facade Research

Ornamental Facade

Investigating the intersection of computational ornamentation and architectural envelope design — generating patterned facade systems that encode cultural, structural, and environmental logic through parametric repetition and variation.

View project →

Project brief

A perforated envelope wraps a compact volume, its diamond-shaped apertures repeating across the surface in a rhythm that filters light while keeping the facade legible as a single, calm mass.

The study treats ornament as a performative layer rather than decoration: pattern density, aperture size and repetition are parameters that can be tuned toward privacy, shade and cultural reference.

02 — Parametric Geometry

Sine Wave Architecture

A structural envelope generated from continuous sine-wave modulation — translating a simple periodic function into undulating architectural surfaces through Grasshopper scripting and AI-driven visualization.

View project →

Project brief

A single continuous shell rises and falls along a sine curve, forming a vaulted white envelope that opens into a fully glazed front. The roof and walls read as one surface rather than separate elements.

Amplitude and frequency of the wave drive the form, so a small change in the underlying function reshapes the whole section — a direct way to test spatial proportion, daylight and entry sequence.

03 — Facade Research

Polygonal Perforation

Inspired by crystal faceted geometries, the facade is constructed upon a logic of Voronoi pattern to develop irregular, unique polygonal perforations — a folded white skin that filters daylight into a commercial volume above a fully glazed ground floor.

View project →

Project brief

Inspired by crystal faceted geometries, the facade is constructed upon a logic of Voronoi pattern to develop irregular, unique polygonal perforations. Each opening grows from a cell of the diagram, so no two apertures share the same shape or size.

Folded facets surround every opening and carry the surface in and out of shadow through the day. The same faceted skin continues inside, shaping the atrium ceiling and bringing daylight down into the floors, while a plain flank wall and a transparent base keep the volume calm at street level.

04 — Material Study

Stratified Travertine

An investigation into travertine as a generative material logic — layering sedimentary strata into an architectural massing study that explores texture, porosity, and light through AI-assisted material visualization.

View project →

Project brief

Stacked, softly offset travertine bands form a mid-rise massing whose plan edge undulates, so each floor plate catches light differently and the building reads like a cut through sedimentary rock.

The massing was first resolved as a clean geometric model, then carried into visualization to study how the stone layering, glazing at the base and surrounding streetscape work together.

06 — Sacred Architecture

Crystal Mosque

View project →

Project brief

09 — Landscape Design

Voronoi Garden

A generative landscape concept built on Voronoi tessellation — using cellular subdivision logic to organize planting beds, pathways, and shaded thresholds into an organic garden composition.

View project →

Project brief

A public square organised by Voronoi cells: some become lawns, some paving, and some shallow water, with paths following the cell edges. The result is a garden that feels irregular but is governed by a single rule.

The study starts from a plan diagram that assigns each cell a function, then follows it through to aerial and eye-level views of the finished composition.
