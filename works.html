Selected Works

Projects

Bridging creative vision with contemporary architectural tools — a curated selection of work across conceptual design, AI visualization, and computational research.

01 — Computational Systems

Grid

A study of the architectural grid as both structure and surface — using parametric manipulation to push a fundamental ordering system toward spatial complexity, material depth, and perceptual richness.

02 — Generative Surface

Layered Flow

Multiple flow fields layered and intersected to produce emergent surface geometries — an investigation into how simple rule-based systems generate spatially complex, atmospheric architectural forms.

03 — Facade Research

Ornamental Facade

Investigating the intersection of computational ornamentation and architectural envelope design — generating patterned facade systems that encode cultural, structural, and environmental logic through parametric repetition and variation.

04 — Parametric Geometry

Sine Wave Architecture

A structural envelope generated from continuous sine-wave modulation — translating a simple periodic function into undulating architectural surfaces through Grasshopper scripting and AI-driven visualization.

05 — Material Study

Stratified Travertine

An investigation into travertine as a generative material logic — layering sedimentary strata into an architectural massing study that explores texture, porosity, and light through AI-assisted material visualization.

06 — Landscape Design

Voronoi Garden

A generative landscape concept built on Voronoi tessellation — using cellular subdivision logic to organize planting beds, pathways, and shaded thresholds into an organic garden composition.

07 — Conceptual Form

Fluid Chand

Chandelier Geometry that highlights beautiful interior scenery while adding luxurious presence.

08 — Sacred Architecture

Crystal Mosque

A faceted reinterpretation of mosque typology, where crystalline geometry replaces conventional massing to modulate light, structure, and spiritual atmosphere — developed through parametric faceting and AI-driven visualization.

09 — Facade Research

Modular Tile Facade System

A modular tiling system for architectural envelopes, studying repetition, variation, and assembly logic at facade scale — bridging parametric tile generation with AI-assisted material and lighting studies.

10 — Urban Furniture

Green Bench

A study in integrating vegetation and seating into a single piece of urban furniture — exploring how planting, shade, and rest can be combined at a human scale within public and landscape settings.
