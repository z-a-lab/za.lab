Ongoing Inquiry

Research

Z.A Lab maintains an ongoing research practice examining alternative design methods and techniques through the fusion of computational design with generative AI tools. Rather than treating algorithmic modelling and machine intelligence as separate stages, the work tests how they can operate as a single continuous process — where geometric logic, visual interpretation, and real-time feedback inform one another throughout the design act.

These studies are design explorations and generative outputs: investigations into method rather than finished building proposals. Findings from this research feed directly into the studio’s project work, visualization workflows, and teaching.

01 — Form-Finding

Form-Finding Approach

Constructing and evolving geometric systems within Grasshopper, then refining them through fine-tuned AI models. This workflow bridges algorithmic generation with intelligent visual interpretation, producing architectural forms that exceed conventional design methods.

02 — Real-Time AI Rendering

Re-Imagining the Computational Design Process

Integrating AI-powered real-time rendering directly within the computational design process — enabling dynamic visual feedback during form exploration and igniting deeper conceptual studies.

03 — Generative AI × Computational Design

Layered Flow

An investigation into where generative AI meets computational design workflows. Multiple flow fields are layered and intersected in a parametric model to produce emergent surface geometries, which are then interpreted through generative AI to develop rich, contextually relevant design schemes. The rule-based system supplies spatial structure; the AI layer adds atmosphere, material and context — so each informs the other as the scheme evolves.

04 — MCP × Design Process

Integrating MCP into the Design Process

Connecting an AI assistant directly to modelling software through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), so a written design brief can be turned into working architectural documents. Within a single workflow, plans, sections and 3D models are generated and refined in the modelling environment, then carried into visualization — shortening the distance between an initial idea and a coordinated set of drawings and views.
